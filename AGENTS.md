# Project Rules

- The hero portrait is a background-removed WebP at `src/assets/seam-portrait.webp` with its lower edge faded out in the file itself, and it must never be framed or show its original light-grey studio background. On desktop (lg and up) it stays anchored to the bottom edge of the `#home` section; below that it is hidden in the hero and instead shown once in the `#contact` section, directly after the "Let's Connect" heading. Why: the source photo is a black-and-white headshot on a light grey studio background, so any framed or un-faded placement reads as a bright rectangle pasted onto the near-black page, and small framed placements in the hero read poorly on phones.
- On desktop the hero headline and the portrait are one composition: the headline sits *behind* the portrait so its last letters tuck a few pixels under the hair, and both share the same lean-in and gentle breathing motion (identical duration and offset) so they move as a unit. Never add a separate speech bubble, label, or connector line between them — the overlap is the connection. Why: a bubble duplicated the headline and a straight connector read as a stray mark.
- Keep "SEAM ZAMAN" on a single line at every desktop width (two headline lines total). The photo column is 380px at lg and 500px at xl, the headline is `text-7xl` at lg and `text-8xl` from 1150px up; the portrait is absolutely positioned in its grid cell so it cannot inflate the section height. Why: a narrower text column or a larger headline wraps the name to three lines and pushes the buttons below the fold.
