# Project Rules

- The hero portrait is a background-removed WebP at `src/assets/seam-portrait.webp` with its lower edge faded out in the file itself, and it must never be framed or show its original light-grey studio background. On desktop it stays anchored to the bottom edge of the `#home` section; on phone it sits small at the left of the name, bottom-aligned, with its faded edge sinking into the page. Why: the source photo is a black-and-white headshot on a light grey studio background, so any framed or un-faded placement reads as a bright rectangle pasted onto the near-black page.
