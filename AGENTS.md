# Project Rules

- The hero portrait is a background-removed WebP at `src/assets/seam-portrait.webp` with its lower edge faded out in the file itself, and it must never be framed or show its original light-grey studio background. On desktop (lg and up) it stays anchored to the bottom edge of the `#home` section; below that it is hidden in the hero and instead shown once in the `#contact` section, directly after the "Let's Connect" heading. Why: the source photo is a black-and-white headshot on a light grey studio background, so any framed or un-faded placement reads as a bright rectangle pasted onto the near-black page, and small framed placements in the hero read poorly on phones.
